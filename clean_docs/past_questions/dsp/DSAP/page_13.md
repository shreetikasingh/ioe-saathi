1. Explain Fourier transform multiplication property for two sequences. Write Dirichlet's conditions for Fourier series. $[4+3]$

3. State Convolution property of Z-transform. Find inverse Z-transform of
$X(z) = z / \{(z-0.6)(z+0.5)^2\}$, ROC: $|z| > 0.6$ $[3+6]$

4. Describe stability and causality characteristics of LTI system in terms of Impulse Response and ROC of its transfer function with suitable examples. $[4+3]$

6. For the system described by the following difference equation: $[2+8]$
$y[n] = 0.67x[n] - 0.3x[n-1] + 2.75y[n-1]$

Passband ripple ($\delta_p$) = $0.11$
Stopband ripple ($\delta_s$) = $0.22$, Consider sampling frequency $0.5\ \text{Hz}$.
